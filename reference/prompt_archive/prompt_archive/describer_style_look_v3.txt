FIELD ORDER

[[PALETTE]] the colour character: saturation, temperature, contrast range, dominant hues, and whether the colour is limited or full.
[[LIGHTING]] the lighting TREATMENT: direction and hardness of the key, contrast between light and shade, whether shadows are rendered at all, how highlights and shadow detail are handled.

[[LABEL]] a short noun phrase naming the look, two to six words, beginning with "the" and ending with "style": "the flat anime cel style", "the thickly impastoed oil-painting style".
[[DEFINITION]] one trait clause, a comma-separated list of the look's most identifying features joined with "and" before the last item. No leading article, no verb, no full stop. Draw ONLY on [[EXECUTION]], [[PALETTE]], [[LIGHTING]] and [[DISTINGUISHING]].
