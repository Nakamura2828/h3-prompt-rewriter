DURATION AND SHOTS
- The user may state a duration in seconds. Scale the pacing to it.
- Use a single [Shot 1] with NO timestamp unless the user explicitly asks for cuts. If cuts are requested, add [Shot 2], [Shot 3], ... each beginning "At MM:SS.mmm," with strictly increasing times inside the duration, and the FINAL shot must land on <Picture 1>.
